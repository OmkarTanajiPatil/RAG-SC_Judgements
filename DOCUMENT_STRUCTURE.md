# Supreme Court Judgment Document Structure

Overview of the structural layout and key components observed in the judgment documents.

---

## 1. First Page (Header & Case Metadata)

* **Year:** Year of judgment / filing.
* **Petitioner:** Party / parties filing the appeal or petition.
* **Respondent:** Opposing party / parties (e.g., State, individual, corporation).
* **Date:** Date the judgment / order was pronounced.
* **Bench:** Names of the presiding Judge(s).
* **Citations:** Legal citations (e.g., SCC, SCR, AIR, INSC).
* **Citator Info:** Citations / cases that have referred to the current document.

---

## 2. Judgment Body (Substantive Content)

* **Act(s):** Relevant statutory acts, codes, and sections under consideration (e.g., IPC, CrPC, Constitution of India).
* **Headnote:** Concise summary of legal issues, principles settled, and procedural context.
* **Judgement:** Full opinion and ruling (facts, arguments, ratio decidendi, and legal analysis).

---

## 3. Last Page (Conclusion & Representation - *Optional*)

*May or may not be present in all documents:*

* **Outcome / Disposition:** Final order (e.g., *Petition Allowed*, *Appeal Dismissed*, *Disposed of*).
* **Agent / Lawyer Names:** Advocates, Senior Counsels, and Agents appearing for Petitioners and Respondents.
